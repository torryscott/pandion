Workshop · Task 7 of 7

Extras and the take-home.

Showing

Done early, or wrapping up? Both paths are here.

Stretch tasks, in any order:

  1. Preview your figure under color-blind vision: Chart settings → Accessibility. Click the tiles; watch the whole chart re-render through someone else’s eyes.
  2. In the Omnibus table, click the η² value and read its plain-language card. Every statistic in the app explains itself this way.
  3. Open Which graph? (in the ? panel) and read the Bar card’s trade-off line: the app is honest about what each chart type hides.
  4. This same table holds a semester of stress ratings (stress_wk1, stress_wk6, stress_wk12). Switch Analysis to Repeated Measures, hand it the three stress columns, and add Connect subjects from the + menu: every student’s own semester.The repeated-measures guide shows the setup; every student’s own semester drawn behind the means.

The take-home: bring one of your own datasets and make one honest figure with it. If the right analysis is not obvious, start with Help me choose. The guides are the narrated version of everything you discovered today, and the statistics guide picks up exactly where task 5 left off.

That is the workshop. Data in, chart drawn, tested honestly. Every edit along the way was one of three rules: click to change, drag to move, press + to add. Everything else is depth on the same moves.
