Workshop · Task 2 of 7

Help me choose.

Showing

Before building anything, meet the guide that answers the “which chart?” question. Hand it the exact variables you are wondering about and it names the analysis, with reasons.

  1. Click the + beside the chart tabs at the top. The New chart dialog opens: seven analyses, each named by the question it answers, and Help me choose at the end.
  2. Click Help me choose, then Use my variables.
  3. Click study_method and exam_score in the variable list. The recommendation appears as you pick: Compare Groups, with a plain sentence on WHY those two variables make it a group-comparison design, and good starting chart types.
  4. Read the second card too. The guide also names another interpretation (Distribution) and says when you would want that instead. It is reasoning with you, not answering at you.
  5. Close the dialog with Close. (The Create Compare Groups chart button would draw it for you, and with your own data that is the everyday shortcut. Today, close it: the next task builds the same chart by hand so you meet the machinery.)

No data yet? The Answer questions mode on the same dialog gets to the same recommendation from a few plain-language questions.

  1. On the Plots ribbon tab, open Pandion Plots and choose Help Me Choose, at the bottom of the menu under Guide.
  2. Drop study_method and exam_score into its Variables box.
  3. Read the recommendation cards in the results: the analysis that fits those two variables (Compare Groups), a plain sentence on WHY, and the graph types worth starting with. A second card names another interpretation and says when you would want it instead.

With no variables in the box, the same analysis runs as a question wizard instead. Either route ends at a named analysis with the reason why; you then open that analysis from the menu yourself, which is exactly the next task.

Checkpoint: the wizard names Compare Groups for this pair and tells you why. That is the chart you build next.
