Workshop · Facilitator

Running the orientation.

The companion to the participant task sheet: the clock, the three live demos, the known stuck points with their rescues, and the pre-session email. The session teaches by doing; your job is mostly to start it, keep time, and rescue quickly. It works the same whether the room holds colleagues at an orientation or students in a course; pick the platform below, and the task sheet follows the same choice.

Session platform

Before the session

The whole risk of a jamovi session is the first ten minutes, so setup happens by email, days ahead. The readiness check is the load-bearing sentence: anyone who cannot see the module button gets fixed before the day, and anyone you cannot fix uses the browser app and loses nothing.

A browser-app session has almost no setup risk: nothing installs, so the email is short and the readiness check is just opening a link. Locked-down lab machines and Chromebooks are fine; the app runs entirely in the browser and uploads nothing.

Subject: Before Friday’s Pandion Plots session (5 minutes of setup) Three things before the session, so we can spend it making charts rather than installing software: 1. Install jamovi (current version) from jamovi.org if you do not have it. 2. Install the Pandion Plots module: download the module file for your machine from pandionplots.com/download.html, then in jamovi open Modules (top right), choose jamovi library, and use the sideload arrow to add the downloaded .jmo file. 3. Readiness check: open jamovi and confirm a Pandion Plots button appears on the Plots ribbon tab. If it does not, reply to this email and we will sort it before the session. Also download the practice dataset we will use: pandionplots.com/assets/tutorial/sample-student-wellbeing.csv No jamovi on your machine? No problem: the browser version at pandionplots.com/app needs no install, and everything in the session works there too.
Subject: Before Friday’s Pandion Plots session (2 minutes of prep) Nothing to install for this one. Two things before the session: 1. We will use the browser version: pandionplots.com/app Open it once so you know it loads on your machine; you should see a welcome screen. Any current browser works. 2. Download the practice dataset we will use (or use the built-in Student wellbeing survey example on the welcome screen): pandionplots.com/assets/tutorial/sample-student-wellbeing.csv Bring a laptop if you can; pairs are fine. Your data never leaves your machine, so locked-down or shared computers are not a problem.

Day-of checklist: the workshop URL on the board or in chat (one task per page with a Next button; the overview lists them all, so latecomers can jump in), the target figure ready to project during task 4, and one machine of yours with BOTH jamovi and the browser app open. Any page prints cleanly if you want paper copies of a task.

The 60-minute clock

WhenWhatYou
0–5 Frame it; everyone opens the CSV. One sentence of pitch, then demo 1 (below). Resist the tour; the tasks are the tour.
5–10 Task 1: fix the exam_score column’s measure type. Task 1: open the example and inspect the exam_score variable. Say out loud WHY it imported as Nominal: this is the stumble their students will hit. Show the Measure type menu in the inspector: their own data will not always import this cleanly.
10–14 Task 2: Help me choose reads the two variables. Let the wizard do the talking. The beat to land: it names Compare Groups and says WHY.
14–19 Task 3: first chart. Circulate. Anyone without bars by minute 18 gets rescued, not narrated to.
19–34 Task 4: recreate the target figure. Project the target. Give the room the first two rules when energy dips: click the thing you want to change, drag the thing you want to move.
34–45 Task 5: omnibus and brackets. Demo 2 as the segment opens. The brackets-landing-on-the-figure moment is the one that sells; let it land.
45–52 Task 6: truncate the axis, get caught, fix it. Frame it: the checker is on their students’ charts too.
52–58 Task 7 stretch items for fast finishers. Use the slack to finish rescues.
58–60 Close. Demo 3: the Learn pages and their platform toggle. State the take-home: one honest figure from their own data.

The three live demos, two minutes each

Everything else is their hands, not yours.

1. The three rules (minute 3). On a drawn chart, say each rule as you perform it. Click a bar, then the y-axis title (want to change it? click it). Drag a bar into a new spot (want to move it? drag it). Press + and flick Data points on and off (is something missing? press +). Close with the point of it all: there is no settings maze; the chart is the menu.

2. The Σ panel (minute 34). Open it, hover a comparison row so the chart rings the bars it refers to, and point at the green p chips. Ten seconds on the Matches the chart badge: these numbers recompute whenever the chart changes.

3. Where help lives (minute 58). The Learn page, the platform toggle on the guides, and the glossary popovers (click any statistic anywhere).

Stuck points and rescues

In rough order of likelihood. Rescue with one sentence and move on; the sheet does the rest.

No Pandion button in jamovi. Module not installed or old jamovi. Do not debug live: switch them to the browser app and have them flip the sheet’s toggle.

exam_score refuses the value axis. They skipped task 1; the column is still Nominal. Double-click the column heading, Measure type, Continuous.

A variable refuses a role. Its measure type is wrong for the slot (usually numbers that came in as text, in their own data). Click the column heading; the inspector’s Measure type menu fixes it.

“Is this uploading our data?” No: the app runs entirely in the browser and the data stays on the machine. Worth saying out loud once in any room with IT anxieties.

“Where is the setting for X?” The universal answer: click X itself. Titles, bars, error bars, the legend, axis numbers, all of it.

Styling changed one bar, not all of them. The Applies to switch at the panel’s top right chooses the clicked bar versus all bars.

Place brackets does nothing. Nothing was ticked. The checkboxes choose which comparisons get placed.

A panel seems stuck open. Esc steps out one layer at a time; a click on empty chart space also settles things.

The data points are missing (task 4). Nudge toward the + menu without naming the item: “anything not on the chart yet gets added from there.”

Choices made on purpose

For when a colleague asks why the session runs this way.

jamovi-first because that is the teaching platform, and the jamovi-specific steps (module install, measure types, the options panel) are exactly the ones worth doing once with help in the room; the chart editor itself is identical everywhere. Pairs because exploration stalls less with two people guessing. No bring-your-own-data inside the session: it would crowd the recreation task, and as the take-home it gives the session a follow-up instead of an ending.

Browser-first because it removes every install risk from a room you may not control, and the chart editor is identical to the jamovi module, so nothing taught here needs relearning if they move to jamovi later.
