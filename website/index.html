Clear statistical figures without the struggle.

Pandion Plots is a free, open-source graphing app for researchers, instructors, and students. Import your data, assign variables, and create a polished statistical figure without code. Everything runs locally, so your data stays on your computer.

Beyond the chart

Pandion supports what comes before and after the chart. Organize and understand your data before you plot, keep a record of what you find as you go, then arrange finished charts into a polished figure for a paper, poster, or presentation.

Two-minute tour

The Data workspace

What the tour shows

The tour stops at nine steps for you to do, and plays the rest on its own. With the sound on it is narrated throughout, with captions, and each stop's card is read aloud. Show me does any step for you, and Just watch plays the whole tour straight through.

This chart is drawn from a sheet of data, and the Data workspace is where it lives. To bring your data in, open a file or paste cells straight from a spreadsheet. Each column is a variable; each row is one case.

Inspect: click a column's heading and its properties open on the right: its measure type, and a summary of its values. The icon beside the name lists all four measure types, and a variable's type decides where it can go in a chart. Scores are numbers you can average, so that column stays Continuous.

Compute: right-click a heading and choose Add computed column. The Functions list has ready-made recipes; pick z-score, choose the column it needs, and the formula is written for you. The preview shows the first few results. Save the variable, and the new column updates itself whenever your data changes.

Filter: click Filter and choose a column and a level (here, only the rows where site is East), then apply the filter. The other rows are dimmed, but nothing is deleted: they are simply left out of every chart and statistic, and the chart notes the filter underneath.

Inspect every column. Compute new variables. Filter without deleting.

Two-minute tour

The Notebook

What the tour shows

The tour stops at nine steps for you to do, and plays the rest on its own. With the sound on it is narrated throughout, with captions, and each stop’s card is read aloud. Show me does any step for you, and Just watch plays the whole tour straight through.

Charts change while you explore. The Notebook keeps what you find, just as it was. It lives in the sidebar, and everything you keep lands there as a page, with the date you kept it and room for your notes.

Keep it: click Stats to see the comparisons, then click the one you want, and its two bars get rings. Click Keep. The chart, its rings, and its statistics are kept together, as one page. You can also right-click any chart and choose Keep to Notebook.

Keep both: days later, the chart has become a box plot. Click Notebook in the sidebar. Your page is just as you kept it, and it tells you the chart has changed. Click the page to see its details: when it was kept, its statistics, and where it came from. Then click Keep an updated copy. The original stays, and the new version lands right below it. Add a note to say why.

Export it: click Export Notebook, then choose Entire notebook. Pick PDF; each page keeps its details and your notes. Then click Export. You get one page for every kept moment, ready to share.

Keep what you find. Keep both versions. Export the notebook.

Two-minute tour

The Layouts workspace

What the tour shows

The tour stops at ten steps for you to do, and plays the rest on its own. With the sound on it is narrated throughout, with captions, and each stop’s card is read aloud. Show me does any step for you, and Just watch plays the whole tour straight through.

Many charts, one figure. Click Layouts in the sidebar. A layout puts your charts together on one page, at the size you’ll publish it.

Start from a template: click Create layout. The gallery has eight templates to start from. Pick Four-panel grid; each panel gets a chart, lettered A to D. Then click Create layout. The figure is built on a page at its real size, ten and a half by seven inches. Use the toolbar to add more charts, text, images, or panel labels.

Drag to arrange: drag a panel, and its letter comes along. Smart guides line it up with the other panels. Drag it back, and it snaps into place. You can turn guides and snapping on or off.

Panels stay live: right-click panel D, and choose Show live chart in Charts. Click Bar to change the chart type, and pick Box. Then click Layouts to go back: panel D has followed, and shows the box plot too. When your figure is ready, click Export layout. Pick PDF, and you get one sharp page, ten and a half by seven inches.

Start from a template, drag to arrange, and your panels stay live.
