Publication-ready charts. Research-grade statistics.

Pandion Plots is a free graphing app for research, analysis, and teaching. Drop in your variables and get a figure worth publishing, with the statistics to back it up. Click any part of the chart to refine it. No accounts, no uploads: your data never leaves your machine.

Why Pandion

A professional tool that explains itself.

Pandion Plots is built for the whole arc of quantitative work: explore the data, check assumptions, and produce the figure that goes in the manuscript. It was built by a professor who teaches statistics, so every statistic can explain itself in plain language. Novices lean on the guidance; experts never notice it.

Beautiful defaults

Drop in one or two variables and get a polished, publication-ready chart with zero configuration.

Edit on the chart

Click any part of the chart to style it: bars, axes, labels, legends. No hunting through menus.

Research-grade statistics

Comparisons, corrections, and effect sizes verified against R, with APA-formatted results and plain-language readings one click away.

An honest check on your graph

Built-in checks flag truncated axes, misleading colors, and other pitfalls before your figure goes out.

Private by design

Everything runs on your machine. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. Your data stays yours.

Publication figures

Compose multi-panel layouts and export SVG, PNG, JPG, or vector PDF, with captions that travel with the figure.
