Support the project

If you would like to help.

Pandion Plots is free and open source, and it will stay that way. It does however take time and money to keep going, so if it saves you some of both, please feel free to chip in.

What support makes possible

Developing Pandion Plots takes time beyond writing the app itself. Each release also involves testing, documentation, compatibility work, and responding to the people who use it.

Development and testing

Improving the app, investigating problems, and testing each release across its different formats.

Documentation and teaching

Creating clear guidance, examples, and learning resources for researchers, instructors, and students.

Long-term maintenance

Keeping Pandion compatible, dependable, and freely available as the tools around it change.

What will not change

Pandion Plots will remain free and open source. Contributions will never unlock hidden features, priority access, or a different edition of the app, and nothing here is a condition of using it.

Additional ways to support Pandion Plots

None of these cost anything.

  1. Report a bug

    Genuinely valuable, and often more useful than money. A clear report is the difference between a problem I can fix in an evening and one I never see. Say what you did, what happened, and where you were running Pandion.

    Report a bug or email contact@pandionplots.com

  2. Tell a colleague

    Pandion has no marketing budget and no telemetry, so word of mouth is how anyone finds it. If it would help someone in your lab, your department, or your class, pass the link along.

    or share it by email

  3. Ask for a feature

    If there is something you wish Pandion did, or something that got in your way, say so. Rough ideas are as welcome as precise ones, and you do not need to know how the software works to suggest something. A lot of what is in the app now started as somebody saying it was missing.

    Suggest a feature or email contact@pandionplots.com

  4. Try things before they ship

    If you are willing to run a new build now and then and tell me what feels wrong, that is a real help. There is no commitment and no schedule. Email me and I will let you know when something is worth a look.

    Volunteer to test
