About

Built for the lab and the classroom.

Pandion Plots is a free, open-source app for making clear, customizable statistical figures without writing code. Use it as a desktop app, in a browser, as a portable HTML file, or inside jamovi. Instead of working through layers of menus, click the chart itself to change its labels, colors, axes, annotations, and layout.

Pandion Plots was created by Torry Scott Dennis, PhD, an associate professor of neuroscience and psychology, for two parts of his work that kept asking for the same tool: producing research figures and teaching students to understand data visualization.

The project began as Plot Studio in response to a practical problem. Many point-and-click tools are easy to start but difficult to refine. Highly customizable tools often require users to learn a programming language before making their first figure. Pandion Plots is designed for the space between: approachable, direct editing with enough control to produce polished research figures.

Every new chart starts with a considered default and remains open to adjustment. Statistics appear beside the figure and are explained in plain language. The aim is not simply to produce better-looking graphs, but to help people understand what those graphs show and communicate their findings clearly.

Why "Pandion"?

Pandion is the genus of the osprey and the source of the app’s name. The osprey sees the whole surface before choosing where to dive, a fitting symbol for thoughtful analysis: take in the full picture, then focus on what matters. The name is also a nod to Solomon the Seahawk, the osprey mascot of St. Mary’s College of Maryland, where Torry teaches.

Free, private, open

Pandion Plots runs entirely on your device: no accounts, uploads, or telemetry. Privacy is part of the design, not an add-on. The project is open source and developed on GitHub, where bug reports and suggestions are welcome.

Help and support

The user guide covers the chart editor in detail, including the toolbar, styling tools, statistics panel, exporting, and troubleshooting. If something looks wrong or the guide does not answer your question, please open an issue on GitHub. Include your Pandion version, where you ran it (desktop app, browser, portable file, or jamovi), and the steps needed to reproduce the problem.

Pandion Plots is free and open source. If it saves you time and you would like to help with its continued development and maintenance, you can learn about supporting the project. Contributions are entirely optional and do not unlock additional features.

Design principles

  • The first draft should be presentable.
  • The numbers behind a figure must hold up.
  • Never change anyone's data invisibly.
  • Explain everything in plain language.
  • Your data never leaves your machine.
